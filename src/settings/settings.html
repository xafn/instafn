Welcome to Instafn

Instafn is a feature-rich mod for Instagram web that allows you to customize your Instagram experience.

Enable features

Browse the categories in the settings and toggle your desired features on or off. Click "Save Changes" to apply your settings.

Made by afn

I wanted to make an extension that I use daily. Please contact me if there are any bugs or feature requests! Made with lots of love by afn.im <3

Privacy & Receipts

Block Story Seen Receipts Prevents automatically marking stories as seen when you view them.
Manual Mark as Seen Button Adds a button next the to like button in stories to manually mark the story as seen.
Block Typing Receipts Blocks typing receipts from being sent to the server in messages.

Profile

Show Follow Status Indicator Displays whether a user follows you or not on their profile page.
Follow Analyzer The analyzer makes a lot of requests to Instagram's servers to get your followers and following. On large accounts (13,000+ connections) this MAY get you rate-limited. Use at your own risk.
Profile Picture Popup Long press a profile picture on a user's profile to see an enlarged view.
Highlight Popup Long press a highlight on a user's profile to see an enlarged view.
Show Date on Post Hover When hovering over a post on a profile page, show the date underneath like and comment counts.

Video & Media

Universal Video Scrubber Adds a video scrubber to every video without native controls, including reels, stories, and videos in the feed. Also shows the current time and duration of the video.
Hold Video for 2x Speed Press and hold a video, or hold the spacebar while the video is focused / on the page, to fast-forward at 2x speed. Let go to return to normal.
Drag Carousel Dots to Scrub Drag across a carousel post's dot indicator to snap-scroll through its images.

Messages

Native DM Themes Uses the actual theme set in your chat as the background and colour scheme, just like on mobile.
Message Logger Logs deleted messages in the extension. View deleted messages by clicking the box button in the messages textbox. Only logs messages when the Instagram website is active.
Double-Tap to Like Messages Double tap a message in direct messages to like it. Works with a changed default like emoji
Quick Reply Adds a keyboard shortcut to reply to the last message. Ctrl/⌘ + ↑ to reply to the last message when the textbox is focused and empty. Press multiple times to navigate up in available messages.
Quick Edit Adds a keyboard shortcut to edit your last message. Ctrl/⌘ + Shift + ↑ to edit your last message when the textbox is focused and empty.

Downloads

Adds download buttons across Instagram that save media at the best available quality.
Enable Downloads
Ask for Quality Before each download, open a dialog to pick the resolution. When off, the highest available quality is always saved.
Ask Where to Save Show the browser's "Save As" dialog for each download instead of saving straight to your Downloads folder.
Posts Adds a download button to the action bar of posts. Carousels have the option to save one or all media.
